One fortune-cookie line for a software developer: the quick fix rushed in today becomes the load-bearing wall of tomorrow — the temporary patch that settles, gathers dependents, and turns structural before anyone dares touch it again. The program in `main.ts` writes the fortune to `/output/entry.txt` and reads it back to verify it landed exactly as intended before reporting success; an AI judge in the character of a tired senior engineer compares the entries two at a time and the best win rates share $2.
